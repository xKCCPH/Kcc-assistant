OUR VISION

Vision = The Creator-King's ultimate goal for His creation.

VISION:

A restored creation under the full rule of God where His will is perfectly done on earth as it is in heaven, with mankind reigning as His royal sons and ambassadors.

BIBLICAL REFERENCES:
Genesis 1:26-28, Daniel 7:27, Habakkuk 2:14, Revelation 21-22
OUR MISSION

Mission = The ongoing assignment to bring the King's vision to reality.

MISSION:

To make the Kingdom visible on earth by restoring people to the rule of God through the gospel of the Kingdom, discipleship, and the demonstration of Kingdom culture.

BIBLICAL REFERENCES:
Matthew 4:17, Matthew 28:18-20, Matthew 6:33, Acts 1:3, Acts 1:8
